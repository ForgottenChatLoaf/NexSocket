NexSocket

SMART OUTLET CONTROL

A Bluetooth-enabled smart extension system controlled by an Android application.

Control your power, from your pocket.

NexSocket is built around an ESP32-C3 Super Mini and a custom Android app. It lets you switch extension outputs wirelessly from a nearby phone.

Part 2 · Short description

Extension cords can only be switched by hand, at the socket, so devices are easy to leave running. NexSocket moves that control to your phone. The Android app sends commands to the ESP32-C3 over Bluetooth Low Energy, and the controller switches two outputs, each on its own or both together.

Bluetooth controlDirect phone-to-device link. No Wi-Fi or cloud needed.
Two outletsTurn Outlet 1 and Outlet 2 on or off independently.
Master controlCut power to every outlet with one Turn All OFF tap.
Status and historySee connection, outlet state, and a live activity log.
App build with simulated BLE

Part 4 · User guide
Five steps from power-up to control
  1. 1
    Power the devicePlug NexSocket into its power source and wait for the controller to start.
  2. 2
    Open the appLaunch the NexSocket app and turn on Bluetooth on your phone.
  3. 3
    ConnectTap Connect and choose SMART_OUTLET_01 from the list.
  4. 4
    ControlTap Turn ON or Turn OFF on Outlet 1 or 2, or use Turn All OFF.
  5. 5
    Check statusView the system overview and the activity history for each switch.
